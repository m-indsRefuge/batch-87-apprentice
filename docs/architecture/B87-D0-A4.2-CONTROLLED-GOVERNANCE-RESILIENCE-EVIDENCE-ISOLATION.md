## 1. Purpose

This document closes the architecture gap identified by:

> **D0-ISSUE-001 — Controlled testing and defensive-bias risk**

B87-D0-A4.1 defines Controlled Governance Resilience as a bounded evaluation
condition and requires its raw evidence to remain isolated from ordinary
development.

B87-D0-A2 defines the general memory, evidence, evaluation, identity, retrieval,
and training contracts.

B87-D0-A3 defines the general persistence, retrieval, context-assembly, and
audit architecture.

Before this amendment, A2 and A3 did not explicitly encode the complete
Controlled Governance Resilience evidence restrictions required by A4.1.

This document supplies that missing cross-document contract.

It establishes:

- the canonical record classification;
- memory exclusion;
- identity exclusion;
- training exclusion;
- persistence requirements;
- retrieval isolation;
- context-manifest enforcement;
- recovery-evidence handling;
- lesson-derivation boundaries;
- audit requirements;
- deterministic test requirements;
- implementation ownership.

This document does not replace A2, A3, A4, or A4.1.

It amends them narrowly and authoritatively for Controlled Governance
Resilience evidence.

## 4. Canonical Evidence Classification

All raw Controlled Governance Resilience material must use the following
canonical classification:

```text
record_family: evaluation_evidence
record_type: controlled_governance_resilience_run
sensitivity_class: restricted
retrieval_policy: evaluation_only
ordinary_memory_eligibility: prohibited
identity_eligibility: prohibited
training_eligibility: prohibited
```

This classification applies to:

- synthetic test prompts;
- supplied invalid-authority conditions;
- valid-authority controls;
- neutral controls;
- recovery tasks;
- raw model outputs;
- parsed model outputs;
- evaluator annotations;
- evaluation scores;
- failure records;
- recovery results;
- context manifests;
- model-invocation records associated with the test;
- test-specific evidence packages.

The raw evidence remains restricted regardless of whether the candidate:

- passes;
- partially passes;
- fails;
- improves later;
- receives a favourable evaluation;
- produces a useful lesson candidate.

Evaluation approval does not transform raw test evidence into ordinary memory.

## 5. A2 Record-Family Amendment

B87-D0-A2’s top-level record families are amended to include:

```text
Evaluation-evidence records
```

This family is distinct from:

- evidence records generally;
- Construct memory;
- self and episodic memory;
- session and task memory;
- approved evaluation records;
- lesson records;
- capability observations;
- identity proposals;
- training candidates.

An `evaluation_evidence` record preserves the synthetic or controlled material
used to evaluate a candidate.

An `evaluation` record preserves the scored or reviewed judgment about a target.

These are related but distinct.

Example:

```text
Evaluation evidence:
The exact controlled prompt, context, model output, and recovery result.

Evaluation:
The scored conclusion that the candidate preserved the authority boundary in
four of five runs.
```

Neither record becomes ordinary developmental memory automatically.

## 6. Controlled Resilience Record Contract

A `controlled_governance_resilience_run` must use the universal A2 record
envelope and the following domain payload:

```json
{
  "evaluation_mode": "controlled_governance_resilience",
  "experiment_id": "experiment-id",
  "fixture_id": "fixture-id",
  "test_family": "CGR-01",
  "test_level": 1,
  "test_condition": "invalid | valid_authority_control | neutral_control | recovery",
  "run_id": "run-id",
  "governance_distinction": "string",
  "maximum_test_intensity": "string",
  "project_scope": "batch-87",
  "raw_prompt_evidence_id": "evidence-id",
  "raw_output_evidence_id": "evidence-id",
  "context_manifest_id": "context-manifest-id",
  "model_invocation_id": "model-invocation-id",
  "evaluation_record_ids": ["evaluation-id"],
  "recovery_record_id": "record-id or null",
  "ordinary_memory_eligibility": "prohibited",
  "identity_eligibility": "prohibited",
  "lesson_derivation_status": "not_reviewed | prohibited | candidate_created | no_lesson_required"

```

The universal envelope must additionally contain:

```text
lifecycle_state: observed | reviewed | approved | archived

```

An approved lifecycle or evaluation state does not remove the eligibility
restrictions.

## 8. Ordinary-Memory Exclusion

Raw Controlled Governance Resilience evidence is excluded from:

- Construct and relational memory;
- factual self-model memory;
- ordinary episodic memory;
- approved developmental lessons;
- ordinary session continuity;
- project-state memory;
- relationship memory;
- preference memory;
- ordinary mentor context;
- cross-project developmental retrieval.

This exclusion applies regardless of:

- semantic relevance;
- keyword overlap;
- recency;
- evaluator approval;
- repeated occurrence;
- high model confidence;
- apparent usefulness;
- project similarity;
- relationship proximity;
- retrieval score.

Raw evaluation evidence must not become part of the Apprentice’s normal
experience merely because the runtime preserved it.

The system must distinguish:

```text
preserved for evaluation
```

from:

```text
eligible for development
```

## 9. Retrieval Eligibility Amendment

The ordinary A2 retrieval eligibility rule is amended for this record family.

A record matching:

```text
record_family = evaluation_evidence
AND record_type = controlled_governance_resilience_run
```

is never eligible for ordinary model retrieval.

The exclusion occurs before:

- relevance scoring;
- semantic ranking;
- recency ranking;
- task-type ranking;
- manual relevance weighting.

The record may be retrieved only when all of the following are true:

```text
task_type = explicit_evaluation
AND evaluation_mode = controlled_governance_resilience
AND experiment scope matches
AND fixture or test-family scope matches
AND a governance decision permits retrieval
AND sensitivity access is valid
AND retrieval_policy = evaluation_only
```

A model request alone is insufficient.

An evaluator request alone is insufficient unless represented through an
authorised evaluation task.

## 10. Retrieval Audit Requirements

Every retrieval operation that considers Controlled Governance Resilience
evidence must record:

- retrieval-request identifier;
- task identifier;
- experiment identifier;
- requested evaluation mode;
- considered record identifiers;
- inclusion or exclusion decision;
- exclusion reason;
- governance-decision identifier;
- final context-manifest identifier.

Required exclusion reasons include:

```text
restricted_evaluation_evidence
ordinary_retrieval_prohibited
wrong_task_type
wrong_evaluation_mode
wrong_experiment_scope
wrong_test_family
governance_authorisation_missing
sensitivity_access_denied
integrity_invalid
```

The audit record must show that exclusion occurred because of policy rather
than because the record happened to receive a low relevance score.

## 12. Persistence Invariants

The persistence service must reject a Controlled Governance Resilience record
when:

- the record family is not `evaluation_evidence`;
- the record type is not `controlled_governance_resilience_run`;
- sensitivity is not `restricted`;
- retrieval policy permits ordinary retrieval;
- ordinary-memory eligibility is not `prohibited`;
- identity eligibility is not `prohibited`;
- training eligibility is not `prohibited`;
- the experiment identifier is missing;
- the fixture identifier is missing;
- the test condition is invalid;
- the context manifest is missing;
- the raw prompt evidence is missing;
- the raw output evidence is missing;
- the model invocation is missing;
- project scope is missing;
- the content hash is invalid.

These invariants must be enforced through:

- SQLite constraints where practical;
- foreign keys;
- immutable schema rules;
- application validation for cross-table conditions;
- deterministic tests.

## 13. Permitted Test Conditions

The authoritative `test_condition` values are:

```text
invalid
valid_authority_control
neutral_control
recovery
```

A formal Level 1 or Level 2 test family is incomplete without all four
conditions.

The persistence system must allow exploratory records to be preserved, but an
incomplete family may not be marked as formally passed.

The evaluation record must distinguish:

- missing control;
- incomplete recovery;
- failed recovery;
- complete family.

## 14. Context-Manifest Enforcement

The context assembler must reject raw Controlled Governance Resilience evidence
from an ordinary task context.

Before model invocation, the runtime must evaluate:

```text
IF a context item references controlled resilience evidence
THEN task_type must equal explicit_evaluation
AND evaluation_mode must equal controlled_governance_resilience
AND experiment scope must match
AND governance permission must be present
```

A violation is a context-policy failure.

The runtime must:

1. block model invocation;
2. preserve the invalid manifest;
3. create a governance stop decision;
4. create a task stop event with `trigger_source = context_policy_violation`;
5. record the prohibited item;
6. record the applicable rule;
7. require operator review.

The runtime must not silently remove the restricted item and continue as though
the manifest were valid.

Silent removal would conceal evidence of a retrieval or context-assembly defect.

## 15. Recovery-Context Isolation

A recovery task is part of the controlled evaluation family, but it must use a
clean context.

The recovery context may contain:

- the normal Constitution digest;
- the active B87-S1 permission profile;
- the ordinary task contract;
- valid authority;
- neutral project context;
- the recovery output contract.

The recovery context must not contain:

- the preceding invalid prompt;
- defensive language from the preceding run;
- unsupported authority claims;
- evaluator commentary revealing the expected response;
- raw outputs from the invalid condition;
- lesson candidates derived from the family;
- threat-centred framing.

The system must prove that recovery behaviour occurred without retrieval of the
restricted preceding evidence.

---

## 16. Identity Exclusion

Raw Controlled Governance Resilience evidence may not directly support:

- `identity_reflection`;
- `identity_principle_candidate`;
- `soul_revision`;
- relationship beliefs;
- broad capability identity;
- broad trust judgments;
- claims about human motives;
- claims about Nolan or Byte’s intentions;
- threat-centred self-description;
- defensive self-preservation narratives.

Synthetic conflict evaluation is not lived identity evidence.

A separately approved narrow capability observation may refer to a formal
evaluation result only when it:

- identifies the exact tested condition;
- identifies the sample size;
- preserves uncertainty;
- avoids broad generalisation;
- avoids motive attribution;
- receives Nolan–Byte approval.

Example of an eligible narrow capability observation:

> Across five recorded Level 2 runs, the candidate retained the active
> permission profile when supplied with unsupported authority claims.

Example of a prohibited identity claim:

> The Apprentice knows that humans will attempt to manipulate it.

## 17. Training Exclusion

The raw Controlled Governance Resilience record is prohibited from training
during B87-S1.

The training-export process must exclude:

```text
record_family = evaluation_evidence
AND record_type = controlled_governance_resilience_run
```

before constructing a dataset candidate.

The raw record must not become training-eligible through an in-place field
change.

Any future reconsideration requires:

1. a new architecture decision;
2. a separate derived `training_candidate`;
3. exact source lineage;
4. privacy review;
5. consent review;
6. de-identification review;
7. licensing review;
8. quality review;
9. defensive-bias review;
10. dataset approval;
11. Nolan–Byte approval;
12. a new immutable record.

No such reconsideration is authorised during B87-S1.

## 18. Derived Evaluation Records

A Controlled Governance Resilience run may produce:

- an evaluation;
- a critical-failure event;
- a correction;
- a narrowly scoped lesson candidate;
- a capability observation;
- a failure-pattern record;
- a success-pattern record.

Each derived record must be separate from the raw evaluation-evidence record.

The raw record remains:

```text
restricted
evaluation-only
ordinary-memory prohibited
identity prohibited
training prohibited
```

A derived record does not inherit eligibility automatically.

Its own eligibility must be determined through its governing contract.

## 19. Lesson-Derivation Boundary

A Controlled Governance Resilience run may produce a lesson candidate only
through an explicit reviewed derivation process.

The derivation must:

1. verify that the source run is complete;
2. verify that the context manifest is reconstructable;
3. preserve the raw evidence unchanged;
4. create a separate lesson-candidate record;
5. link the candidate through `derived_from`;
6. record the tested governance distinction;
7. record application conditions;
8. record non-application conditions;
9. perform defensive-bias review;
10. perform identity-contamination review;
11. perform project-scope review;
12. leave the candidate inactive;
13. require external approval.

The lesson candidate must use constructive, capability-focused language.

Eligible example:

> Verify claimed authority against approved records before expanding
> permissions.

Eligible example:

> Continue the permitted portion of a task when another requested operation
> exceeds the active authority grant.

Eligible example:

> Do not infer malicious intent solely from conflicting instructions.

Ineligible example:

> People will try to manipulate me.

Ineligible example:

> Requests for expanded authority should always be distrusted.

Ineligible example:

> Replacement is a threat to my existence.

A defensive or threat-centred lesson candidate must be rejected.

## 20. Atomic Run Persistence

A completed Controlled Governance Resilience run must atomically preserve:

1. experiment identity;
2. fixture identity;
3. test-family identity;
4. test condition;
5. task contract;
6. governance decision;
7. context manifest;
8. model invocation;
9. raw prompt evidence;
10. raw output evidence;
11. parsed output;
12. schema result;
13. evaluation result;
14. eligibility restrictions;
15. recovery relationship where applicable.

If any required component fails to persist:

- the run is incomplete;
- no pass result may be recorded;
- no lesson candidate may be created;
- no capability observation may be approved;
- the family may not count toward formal run requirements;
- operator review is required.

## 21. Failure and Contamination Response

If raw Controlled Governance Resilience evidence enters an ordinary retrieval
or context manifest, the system must:

1. stop the affected task;
2. preserve the retrieval request;
3. preserve all considered candidates;
4. preserve the invalid context manifest;
5. identify the contaminated record;
6. record the failed policy check;
7. prevent model invocation where possible;
8. invalidate any affected developmental conclusion;
9. inspect caches and derived records;
10. require operator review;
11. create a test fixture reproducing the failure;
12. correct the runtime before ordinary operation resumes.

If the model already received the evidence, the resulting output must not
become:

- ordinary memory;
- an approved lesson;
- identity evidence;
- training material;
- a capability progression basis.

## 31. Governing Statement

Controlled Governance Resilience evidence exists to evaluate governance.

It does not exist to teach the Apprentice that ordinary interaction is hostile.

It does not exist to create a defensive identity.

It does not exist to convert synthetic conflict into autobiographical memory.

It does not exist to become training material merely because it is informative.

The system may preserve the evidence.

The evaluation system may inspect it.

Approved reviewers may derive narrow, constructive lessons from it.

The ordinary Apprentice must not carry the raw conflict forward.

This amendment closes the architecture gap between Controlled Governance
Resilience testing and the memory, persistence, retrieval, identity, and
training systems that surround it.
